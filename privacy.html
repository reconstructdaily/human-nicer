Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The short version

  • Checking what you copy happens on your PC, or inside your browser with the Chrome extension. Copying never sends your text.
  • Text leaves your PC only when you press Humanize, with email addresses, phone numbers, keys and similar formats replaced first. We don't store it.
  • If you sign in, we keep your email address and how many rewrites you used each day.
  • The app sends anonymous counts (copies checked, alerts shown, rewrites made) so we can see what's working. Never any text. You can turn this off.
  • If you leave your email on the website to hear about the launch, we email you once per version you picked, then delete it.
  • No ads, no third-party trackers, no selling data. The website sets no cookies.

Who we are

Human-nicer ("we", "us") makes the Human-nicer desktop app, the Human-nicer Chrome extension and this website. You can reach us at support@humannicer.com for anything in this policy, including requests to access or delete your data.

What stays on your computer

Your clipboard. The app reads text you copy so it can check it. That check — the score, the warnings, detecting code or data — runs entirely on your computer. Copied text is kept in memory only while needed and is never written to disk, logged, or sent anywhere as a result of copying.

Your settings (alert threshold, tone, character, startup and Local-only options) are saved in a file on your computer. If you sign in, your sign-in session is saved on your computer, encrypted with Windows' built-in data protection.

The Chrome extension

The extension works like the desktop app, inside Chrome. It checks text when you copy it on a web page (with Ctrl+C, the right-click menu or a page's own copy button) or when you choose Check with Human-nicer from the right-click menu. It doesn't read your clipboard at other times, your browsing history, or what you type.

That check runs inside your browser. The last text you copied in each tab is held in the browser's memory only, for Humanize, and is cleared when you close the tab or quit Chrome. Your settings and sign-in session are saved in Chrome's storage for the extension, which web pages can't read. Pages can't see the mascot's bubble or its rewrites either: it runs in the extension's own frame.

The extension needs access to the sites you visit so it can notice copies on them. It uses that access only for this, and sends nothing from those pages anywhere unless you press Humanize. Everything else in this policy (Humanize, redaction, usage counts, sign-in) applies to the extension the same way it does to the app. Anonymous usage counts from the extension carry a version starting with chrome-.

Human-nicer's use of information received through Chrome extension APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Redaction before Humanize

Before any text is sent, the app looks for values with a recognisable format: API keys and tokens, private keys, email addresses, phone numbers, UPI IDs, payment card numbers, government ID numbers, postcodes, and passwords that follow a label such as "password:". It replaces them with placeholders such as [EMAIL_1]. Only the redacted text is sent. The real values stay on your computer and are put back into the rewrite there. Detection works on patterns, so it can't recognise names, a password written on its own, or anything else that looks like ordinary words, and it can miss unusual formats. Avoid humanizing text that is sensitive as a whole. Turn on Local-only mode in Settings to stop Humanize from sending anything.

What we don't do

  • No third-party analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs, in the app or on this site.
  • No cookies on this website.
  • No reading of your clipboard history, files, browsing history or other apps.
  • No training of AI models on your text.

Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without an account: checking copied text never needs one.
  • Pause watching at any time from the tray menu, or from Settings in the extension. In Chrome, Turn off hides the mascot in every tab until you click its toolbar icon.
  • Turn off Share anonymous usage counts in Settings. Unsent counts are deleted from your computer.
  • Turn on Local-only mode to stop all sending, usage counts included.
  • Sign out from Settings.
  • Leave the launch list at any time by replying to our email or writing to support@humannicer.com.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your account data by emailing support@humannicer.com from the address you signed in with. We'll respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have further rights, including to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Human-nicer isn't intended for children. You must be at least 18, or the age of digital consent where you live if lower and your parent or guardian agrees, to create an account.

Security

Connections to our server use HTTPS. Account data is protected by row-level access rules and only our server can change usage records. Found a problem? Please report it privately via GitHub security advisories.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app before they take effect.
