<h1 align="center">Human-nicer</h1>

<p align="center">
  Catch the AI tells before you hit send.<br>
  A tiny Windows tray app that checks what you copy and rewrites it to sound like you.
</p>

**What it does**

- Every time you copy text, Human-nicer gives it a 0–100 "sounds AI" score, on your
  machine, instantly.
- It catches what people forget to delete: `[Enter Name]`, "I hope this helps!",
  "Here's the revised version:".
- Press **Humanize** to rewrite it in your choice of six tones. Placeholders stay put
  so you can fill them in before copying.

**Privacy**

- Scoring never touches the network. Copying never sends your text.
- Before Humanize sends text, passwords, API keys, emails, phone numbers, card and ID
  numbers are swapped out on your device and put back afterwards.
- The app sends anonymous counts (copies checked, alerts shown), never text. Turn it off in Settings.
- Local-only mode turns sending off completely.

**Install**

Download `Human-nicer-Setup-<version>.exe` from
[Releases](https://github.com/reconstructdaily/human-nicer/releases/latest). Windows
may show a SmartScreen notice for new apps: choose **More info → Run anyway**. Updates
install automatically after that.
