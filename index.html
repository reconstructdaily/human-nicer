How it works

Nothing to open, nothing to paste into. It works wherever you copy.

Get the Windows app or the Chrome extension, then copy as usual: an email draft, a LinkedIn post, a Slack reply. Code, spreadsheets and logs are recognised and left alone.

2

Get an instant check

A 0–100 score and the exact phrases behind it, worked out on your PC in milliseconds. The mascot only speaks up when something's off.

professional · casual · friendly · concise · confident · formal

Your clipboard stays yours

A tool that reads your clipboard has to earn trust. These aren't settings — they're how the app is built, and they're checked by automated tests on every change.

01

Copying never sends your text

The score is plain code running on your PC with no network access. Your clipboard is never uploaded, logged, or saved to disk.

02

Emails, numbers and keys are held back

Before Humanize sends anything, things with a recognisable format, like email addresses, phone numbers, API keys and card numbers, are swapped for placeholders such as [EMAIL_1] on your PC and put back into the rewrite there. The bubble shows you exactly what will be sent. It can't tell that a plain word is a password or a name, so don't humanize text you wouldn't share.

03

Local-only mode turns sending off

One switch in Settings and Humanize refuses to run and anonymous usage counts stop, so nothing about your text can leave your device by any path. Read the full privacy policy.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The check on every copy is free and unlimited. Humanize needs a free account (sign in with Google) and includes 10 rewrites a day.

Is this an AI detector?

It's a writing check for your own text. The score is a quick estimate based on patterns common in AI output — useful for catching things before you send them, not proof of who wrote something. Please don't use it to judge other people's work.

What gets sent when I press Humanize?

The copied text and the tone you picked. Email addresses, phone numbers, API keys, card numbers and similar formats are replaced with placeholders first; names and ordinary words are sent as written. It goes to our server and to Google's Gemini model to be rewritten, and isn't stored by us. Details are in the privacy policy.

Why does Windows show a warning when I install it?

Windows SmartScreen warns about new apps it hasn't seen many downloads of yet. Choose More info → Run anyway. The warning fades as more people install it.

Does it work on Mac?

The desktop app is Windows 10 and 11 only for now. The Chrome extension works in Chrome on Mac, Linux and ChromeOS too.

Windows app or Chrome extension?

The Windows app checks anything you copy on your PC, in any app. The Chrome extension checks what you copy on web pages in Chrome, so it's the one to pick on a Mac or a work laptop where you can't install apps. Both do the same check and the same Humanize, and one free account works on both.

How do I pause or remove it?

Right-click the tray icon → Pause watching. To uninstall, use Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Human-nicer.
